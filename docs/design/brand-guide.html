Brand guide · v1

Know Meet

Know Meet is the KnowMe meeting room where people, their persistent dots and registered agents meet around one shared memory. This guide covers the mark, colour, type, motion and voice that make it recognisable, and the rules that keep it legible at every size.

01 · Logo

The Gathering mark

An ember core, the meeting's shared memory, with five participants gathered around it. One participant is cyan: an agent at the table with the people. The core is the same ember dot that sits at the heart of the KnowMe K, so Know Meet reads as family.

02 · Construction

Built on a 64-unit grid

Every measurement is a whole number on the grid, so the mark stays crisp when it is scaled to 16, 32 or 1024 pixels.

Measurements

  • Core: radius 11, centred at 32, 32. Ember.
  • Ring: radius 21. Five participant dots of radius 6, every 72°, starting straight up.
  • The agent dot is the second one clockwise, at about two o'clock. Cyan.
  • Gap between core and ring dots: 4 units. Gap between neighbouring dots: about 12 units.
  • Wordmark: Space Grotesk Bold, tracking −2%, “Meet” in ember. Cap height aligns with the ring's horizontal centre band.
03 · Clear space and size

Give it room

Keep clear space on every side equal to the core's diameter (one third of the symbol's height). Nothing else (text, edges, other logos) enters that area.

Clear space
Minimum sizes

Symbol: 16px digital, 6mm print. Lockup: 96px wide digital, 25mm print. Below these sizes use the symbol alone.

05 · Colour

Ink, ember and one cyan agent

07 · Motion

The dot is alive

In the product, the ember core becomes a living orb. Its four states are the brand in motion. Interface transitions stay quick: 150–300ms, ease-out in, ease-in out. All motion stops when the system asks for reduced motion.

ListeningPresent and paying attention. A slow breath.scale 1 → 1.04 · 3.2s ease-in-out
ThinkingRetrieving from memory or meeting knowledge.ring sweep · 0.9s linear
SpeakingTalking in the room. Ripples move outward.ripple ×2 · 1.6s ease-out
PausedNot listening and will not speak until resumed.static · 45% opacity, greyscale
08 · Iconography

One outline family

24-unit grid, 1.75 stroke, round caps and joins. Icons never stand alone for meaning: pair them with a label or an accessible name. No emoji as icons.

09 · Voice

Plain, calm and honest about agents

Know Meet speaks like a good meeting host: brief, specific, and clear about who can see what. Agents are participants, never magic.

Say who sees it

Every privacy-relevant surface states its audience in one line.

Do

“Only you and Marcus see this. Agents and the recording don’t.”

Don’t

“Your messages are secure.”

Name the agent and its job

Agents introduce themselves by name and role, and say what they did.

Do

“Scribe added an action item: issue sandbox credentials before kickoff.”

Don’t

“AI magic captured your insights!”

Explain the fix

Errors say what happened and what to do next, in that order.

Do

“Bucket not found: missing-bucket. Check the name or create it, then test again.”

Don’t

“Something went wrong.”
