Mood board

A calm room with live signals

Know Meet should feel like a well-run meeting room after dark: quiet ink surfaces, people front and centre, and small, warm signals that show when an agent or a dot is listening, thinking or speaking. Nothing glows for decoration. Light means something is happening.

Direction

Meet around what you know.

The meeting is the room; the knowledge is the table; the dot is the light that tells you someone, or something, is paying attention.

The live dot

The ember core of the logo, alive in the room. It breathes while listening and ripples while speaking.

Not this

Anti-references

  • Robots, glowing brains, circuit-board backgrounds
  • Purple gradient washes and neon glows
  • Stock handshake and laptop-smile photos
  • Sparkle icons for “AI”
  • Invented metrics and hype claims
Shape vocabulary

Circles for presence, soft rectangles for structure

Participants are always circles. Controls use 12px corners, tiles 16px, panels 20px. Nothing larger.

Motion

Quick in the interface, slow in the dot

Tap150ms
Panel280ms in · 190ms out
Thinking0.9s sweep
Listening3.2s breath

Interface motion confirms an action and gets out of the way. Only the dot moves on its own, because only the dot is alive.
