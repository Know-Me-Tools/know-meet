01 — Inside the room

What happens in a KnowMe meeting

Every room is a LiveKit room with agents as real participants. They hear the same audio, read the same transcript, and work from the same shared knowledge — so the work after the meeting is already done when it ends.

Persistent dots

A dot that remembers you across every meeting

A dot is your always-on personal agent with its own private memory. Iris joins your calls, recalls what was decided last time, and keeps notes only you can read. Your colleagues bring theirs — Avery’s is Sol.

03 — The system agent

Meet the Concierge

The Concierge is in every room, including guest rooms. It greets people as they arrive, explains what’s happening, answers questions from the live transcript, and tells guests what signing in would add. It’s the only agent guests get.

  • Greets and orients
  • Answers from the live transcript
  • Routes questions to the right agent

How a meeting flows

Four steps, each one a screen you can open now.

    Schedule & invite

    Pick a time, add people by email or phone, and choose which agents join. Invites go out by email, SMS and calendar.

    Schedule form
  2. 02

    Meet with agents

    Add an agent

    Record & transcribe

    The transcript builds as you talk. Start a Room Composite recording when everyone has agreed.

    Summary delivered
