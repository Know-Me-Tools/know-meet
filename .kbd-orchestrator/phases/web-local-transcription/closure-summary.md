revision 6. Three product tasks were completed. The separate evidence task
was cancelled in the implementation ledger because evidence belongs at the
phase boundary. The independent review cleared all four prior findings.

images. Those cases observed actual PGlite search responses, visible gaps
when speech could not be aligned, durable readback after a denied save, and
preservation of the separate WebM after transcript deletion.
