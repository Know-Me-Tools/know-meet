## Why

The lobby has no device pickers (the wr05 gap) and the room cannot change microphone, camera or speaker.

## What Changes

- Lobby pickers for microphone and camera with level meter and preview
- In-room settings for microphone, camera and speaker
- Unsupported speaker choice is explained, not offered

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `device-settings`: Participants choose their devices

### Modified Capabilities

- None
