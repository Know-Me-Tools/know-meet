## Why
Let the requester privately find an exact commitment from the ongoing meeting without interrupting anyone or sending captured speech or derived text to an AI service.

## What Changes
Add explicitly chosen browser-local transcription in one supported desktop Chromium profile; separately consented requester-only intake and visibility; settled timestamped search and reopen/delete in that browser.

## Impact
Do not change the web recorder, existing WebM/history behavior, established release pipeline, guest admission, or any paid runtime. The free phase excludes generative answers, embeddings and vector indexing; they are additive after usable local search. The planned paid LiveKit agent lane is deferred.
